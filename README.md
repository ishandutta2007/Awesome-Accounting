## Top Accounting Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Bookkeeping, Invoicing, Financial Reporting, Multi-Currency, Payroll Integration & Small-to-Midsize Business Accounting*
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Accounting**. These tools help businesses and individuals manage general ledger, accounts payable/receivable, invoicing, expense tracking, bank reconciliation, financial reporting, and tax readiness.

**Examples** include QuickBooks Online, Xero, FreshBooks, Wave, Sage Intacct, Zoho Books, Oracle NetSuite, Kashoo, FreeAgent, and ZipBooks (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: The open-source accounting ecosystem is mature and diverse, ranging from full double-entry ERP modules to lightweight personal finance managers and plain-text accounting systems. This section is heavily expanded with every major active project.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[QuickBooks Online](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/)**  
  The most widely used cloud accounting platform for small and midsize businesses in the US, with deep tax support, large accountant ecosystem, and extensive third-party integrations.

- **[Xero](https://www.xero.com/)**  
  Modern cloud accounting software known for unlimited users on all plans, strong multi-currency support, clean interface, and global appeal.

- **[FreshBooks](https://www.freshbooks.com/)**  
  Invoicing-first accounting platform designed for freelancers and service-based businesses, with excellent time tracking, client management, and payment features.

- **[Wave](https://www.waveapps.com/)**  
  Free (or low-cost) accounting and invoicing software popular with very small businesses and side hustles, offering core bookkeeping and receipt scanning.

- **[Sage Intacct](https://www.sage.com/en-us/sage-intacct/)**  
  Cloud financial management platform aimed at mid-market and growing companies needing multi-entity, advanced reporting, and strong controls.

- **[Zoho Books](https://www.zoho.com/books/)**  
  Affordable, feature-rich accounting software that integrates tightly with the broader Zoho ecosystem (CRM, Inventory, Payroll, etc.).

- **[Oracle NetSuite](https://www.netsuite.com/)**  
  Full cloud ERP with comprehensive accounting modules for mid-market and enterprise organizations requiring multi-subsidiary and advanced financial management.

- **[Kashoo](https://www.kashoo.com/)**  
  Simple, straightforward cloud accounting solution focused on ease of use for small businesses.

- **[FreeAgent](https://www.freeagent.com/)**  
  UK-focused accounting software popular with freelancers, contractors, and small limited companies, with strong tax and Making Tax Digital support.

- **[ZipBooks](https://zipbooks.com/)**  
  Cloud accounting platform offering invoicing, expense tracking, and financial reporting with a clean interface aimed at small businesses.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[ERPNext (Frappe)](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  
  Fully open-source ERP with a robust accounting module covering general ledger, invoicing, payments, multi-currency, financial statements, and multi-company support.

- **[Odoo Community](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  
  Modular open-source ERP whose Accounting app provides double-entry bookkeeping, invoicing, bank reconciliation, assets, and reporting; widely used and highly extensible.

- **[Akaunting](https://github.com/akaunting/akaunting)**  
  Modern, free, and open-source online accounting software designed for small businesses and freelancers, with invoicing, expense tracking, and multi-currency support.

- **[GnuCash](https://github.com/Gnucash/gnucash)**  
  Mature open-source double-entry accounting application suitable for personal and small-business use, available on desktop with reports and scheduling features.

- **[Firefly III](https://github.com/firefly-iii/firefly-iii)**  
  Self-hosted personal and small-business finance manager with double-entry bookkeeping, budgets, rules engine, multi-currency, and a rich API.

- **[Frappe Books](https://github.com/frappe/books)**  
  Simple, modern, offline-first desktop accounting software from the Frappe team, focused on invoicing, expenses, and basic bookkeeping for small businesses.

- **[LedgerSMB](https://github.com/ledgersmb/LedgerSMB)**  
  Open-source double-entry accounting and ERP system with strong multi-currency, inventory, and reporting capabilities aimed at small and medium businesses.

- **[Bigcapital](https://github.com/bigcapitalhq/bigcapital)**  
  Modern open-source accounting and inventory software designed for small and medium businesses with a clean interface and double-entry foundation.

- **[Plain Text Accounting tools](https://github.com/ledger/ledger)**  
  Command-line double-entry systems such as Ledger, hledger, and Beancount (with Fava web UI) that treat accounting data as version-controllable plain text.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Invoice & expense focused**: InvoicePlane and similar self-hosted invoicing tools.
- **Personal finance**: ezBookkeeping and other lightweight self-hosted trackers.
- **Desktop & hybrid**: Manager.io (freeware with strong community use) and various desktop clients.
- **ERP-adjacent**: SQL-Ledger and other long-standing open accounting systems.
- **Automation & AI helpers**: Emerging open-source tools and libraries that assist with categorization, receipt scanning, and reporting.
- Large ecosystem of **bank import scripts**, **reporting templates**, and **integration connectors**.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  
For full business accounting, deploy **ERPNext** or **Odoo Community** as the core.  
For lighter needs, use **Akaunting**, **Firefly III**, or **Frappe Books**.  
Power users and developers often prefer **plain-text accounting** (Ledger/Beancount) combined with Git for auditability.  
All of these can be extended with custom reports, bank feeds, and integrations.
